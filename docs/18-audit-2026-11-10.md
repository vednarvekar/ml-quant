# 18 - End-to-end audit (2026-11-10)

Scope: every module in `src/mlq`, docs 00-17, the data on disk, the registry, the paper-trading setup, and re-runs of the key results with extra checks. Audit scripts were run against the existing data and saved predictions. No source files were changed.

## 1. Verdict in one paragraph
The **engineering and research hygiene are well above typical retail level**: next-open execution, India cost model, null and look-ahead tests, purged walk-forward, Deflated Sharpe, trial registry, point-in-time universe, and a paper broker that replays the backtest exactly. All 45 tests pass. **The edge is not real yet.** The ML swing model has a small, statistically real prediction skill (weekly rank IC 0.046, t = 4.8), but (a) most of that skill is plain 5-day reversal, (b) the portfolios it produces have **no significant alpha once midcap exposure is controlled for**, (c) the headline Sharpe numbers depend heavily on which weekday the rebalance happens to fall, and (d) the futures results carry two look-ahead biases that were not known before (today's F&O list and dividend-adjusted prices). In short, the platform is good but the strategy is not proven. Do **not** move to real money on the current model.

## 2. New findings with numbers (2022-01 -> 2026-11, clean window, 5 bps slippage)

### 2.1 The "ML edge" is mostly midcap beta
Regressing daily strategy returns on Nifty 50, (Midcap 100 - Nifty 50), and (Smallcap 100 - Midcap 100):

| Portfolio | CAGR | Sharpe | Alpha/yr after factors | alpha t | R^2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ML weekly hysteresis k20, delivery | 10.3% | 0.59 | **-3.7%** | -0.9 | 0.82 |
| ML 20-day top-20, delivery | 16.8% | 0.86 | +1.9% | 0.5 | 0.81 |
| EW of the model's own top-150 universe, no model | 13.0% | 0.82 | - | - | - |
| **Nifty Midcap 100 index** (buy an ETF) | **15.7%** | **0.91** | - | - | - |
| Nifty 50 index | 8.0% | 0.62 | | | |

The baseline docs 16-17 compare against Nifty 50 and EW top-100. The right baselines are **the model's own universe equal-weighted** and **the Midcap 100 index**. Neither is clearly beaten.

### 2.2 Results depend on the rebalance phase (the same rule started on a different day)
| Strategy | Sharpe across start phases | Docs reported |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly hyst k20, delivery | 0.59, 0.92, 0.57, 0.72, 0.73 | 0.98 |
| 20-day top-20, delivery | 0.86, 0.71, 0.44, 0.90, 0.91 | 0.95 |
| **Paper config** (futures k10 keep20, Rs 20L) | 1.01, **1.55**, 0.75, 1.14, 1.07 | 1.00 |
| Same futures construction with **random scores** (12 seeds) | mean 0.76 (0.58 - 0.91) | not run |

Doc 17 already noted the weekday effect. It is bigger than noted: a 2x range in Sharpe from timing alone. **Futures with random picks already score Sharpe 0.76** because a long-only bull market plus low futures costs carries everything. The model adds about +0.3 Sharpe over random on average, with alpha t ranging from 0.2 to 3.1 depending on phase. That is suggestive, not proof.

### 2.3 What the model actually learned
- IC 2022+: model **0.046**; plain `-ret5` (5-day reversal) alone **0.041**; momentum 0.013; volatility ~0; distance to 52-week high ~0.
- Rank correlation between the model score and -ret5 is 0.34, and the top decile is average on volatility and 20-day return. The model is "buy short-term losers, with some extra conditioning".
- Top decile beats the median by **47 bps per 5 days**. The delivery round trip costs ~39 bps and futures ~17 bps. This is why only futures survive costs.
- IC by year: 2022 = 0.013 (almost nothing in the bear/chop year), 2023-25 ~0.05-0.06. The signal is regime-dependent.
- Training data before 2022 is survivor-biased (only names that later stayed large). Survivors are exactly the stocks whose dips recovered, so **survivorship bias teaches the model "buy the dip"**. That may inflate the reversal pattern it learned.

### 2.4 Two look-ahead biases in the futures backtests (new)
1. **Today's F&O list is used for all history.** 183 names are F&O today; **27 of them did not even exist as listed stocks in early 2022** (KAYNES, MAZDOCK, DATAPATTNS, IREDA, JIOFIN, HYUNDAI, WAAREEENER ...). Many older names were also only *added* to F&O after they rallied (SEBI eligibility depends on size and turnover). The backtest can buy 2022 futures in the 2022-24 defence/PSU winners that were not tradable as futures at the time. Today's lot sizes have the same problem.
2. **Dividend-adjusted prices.** Upstox history is total-return-like (doc 15). Futures holders do **not** receive dividends, so long futures are overstated by roughly the dividend yield (~1-1.5%/yr). The Nifty 50 benchmark is a price index without dividends, which tilts every comparison in the strategy's favour too.

### 2.5 Universe survivorship (rank 101-150)
Coverage of the point-in-time universe: rank 1-100 96%, **rank 101-150 84%**, 151-200 60%. The model trades top-150. Missing names are not random: within rank 101-150, names *with* data had better next-12-month returns than names without data (median -9% vs -18% from Jan-2022, +20% vs +5% from Jul-2022, +51% vs +34% from Jan-2023). The extra 155 names were downloaded because they were "ever top-100 since 2022", which partly means they grew. This inflates every top-150 backtest.

## 3. Code and plumbing issues
| # | Severity | Issue | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Critical before live** | Futures roll is modelled on the 1st trading day of the month. NSE stock futures expire on the **last Tuesday** and are **physically settled**. A live system following this logic takes delivery: full cash needed, plus delivery STT (~0.1%) on the full value. | Roll 2-3 trading days before expiry, using an expiry calendar from the instrument master. |
| 2 | High | Futures P&L uses spot prices, with no basis/carry. The idle cash earns nothing in the sim. | Use the NSE F&O bhavcopy (free, 2000+) for real futures closes, OI, and **historical F&O membership and lot sizes**. That fixes 2.4.1 as well. |
| 3 | High | Fills at the daily open with 5 bps slippage. Open prints on midcaps are noisy, and the pre-open auction price is not guaranteed. | You already have 5-minute data from 2022: backtest fills at the 09:15-09:45 VWAP and compare. |
| 4 | High | Paper trading runs one phase, and 40 days is statistically meaningless for a Sharpe ~1 strategy (standard error of the annual Sharpe over 40 days is ~2.5). | Treat paper as a **plumbing and fill-quality test**, not as edge validation. Log the live quote at decision and execution time (Upstox market-quote API) to measure real slippage. |
| 5 | Medium | `paper.run` rebuilds the full feature cube (5,400 days x 255 names x 21 features, float64 ~ 230 MB per copy, several copies) and re-downloads/rebuilds all data every day. | Incremental features (last ~260 days only), float32, a Parquet feature store. Daily run should take seconds and <300 MB. |
| 6 | Medium | The scheduled task is "Interactive only": it does not run if you are logged out or the laptop is asleep. The 17:30 run can also race NSE bhavcopy publication. | Run with "whether user is logged on or not", run at ~19:30, and alert (Telegram/email) on failure or stale data. |
| 7 | Medium | Margin flat 20%. Midcap stock futures are often 25-40% (SPAN + ELM), and SEBI's 50% cash-collateral rule applies. | Pull margins from the Upstox margin API at decision time; this matters at >1x exposure. |
| 8 | Low | `intraday_strats.pick_symbols` picks names by the **latest** month's liquidity, which is mild look-ahead. | Use the point-in-time universe per day. |
| 9 | Low | No F&O ban-list or circuit-limit handling (you cannot open new positions in banned names). | Ban list from NSE daily file; skip those names. |
| 10 | Process | Each new run uses the previous result as its baseline ("beats EW top-100"). | Fixed baseline set for every report: universe EW, Midcap 100 / Nifty 500, random-score same construction, 5-day reversal rule. Report mean +/- range over all phases, plus factor alpha t-stat. |

## 4. Problems with the plan itself

**4.1 "Multiple small fine-tuned models" is right in spirit and wrong in where the models go.** What big quant firms actually run is mostly many weak **tabular** signals (GBDT/linear, MBs in size, ms inference), unique **data**, careful **costs and execution**, and a **risk/portfolio layer**. Language and foundation models are used to turn **text into structured features**, not to decide trades. Evidence check:
- Time-series foundation models: an independent 2025 study finds off-the-shelf TSFMs (Chronos etc.) weak zero-shot on stock returns; only finance-pretrained ones help, and no independent stock-return test of Kronos exists yet (its gains are the authors' own benchmarks).
- LLM news sentiment: real signal in US studies, but memorisation/look-ahead inflates backtests materially (Gao-Jiang-Yan 2025). Any LLM feature must be from a model whose training cut-off is **before** the test period, or validated only forward in time.
- Your own data already says the binding constraint is **information and cost**, not model capacity. A 21-feature LightGBM learned mostly reversal; a bigger model on the same OHLCV would learn the same thing more expensively.

**4.2 The objective is mis-specified.** The label is the 5-day return relative to the median, and the portfolio is long-only, so most of the P&L is market and midcap beta. Train and evaluate on the **residual** (market-, size- and sector-neutral) return, and run the portfolio **hedged with Nifty futures** (long stocks, short index). Then any Sharpe left is skill.

**4.3 Horizon versus cost.** At 5 days the signal (47 bps) barely beats delivery costs. Signals that pay for India's STT are 10-60 day horizons (earnings drift, flows, quality/value) or event-driven.

**4.4 Intraday.** Correctly diagnosed in doc 17: gross 7-9 bps against ~16 bps costs. Do not invest more here as an alpha source. Reuse the 5-minute data as an **execution model** (when to enter the swing trades in the first hour), where saving 5-10 bps per fill is a direct win.

**4.5 "No false alerts."** Precision comes from fewer, higher-conviction trades with a calibrated confidence and a regime filter (IC was ~0 in 2022). It does not come from a more complex model. But thresholding also cuts the number of bets and raises variance, so confidence must be validated out-of-sample like any other signal.

## 5. Where real, India-specific edge is most likely (data the big shops use, available free)
| Source | What it gives | Why it may work |
|---|---|---|
| **NSE CM bhavcopy `DELIV_PER`** (you already download `sec_bhavdata_full`) | Daily delivery % per stock | Classic Indian accumulation signal; zero new download for 2024+ |
| **NSE F&O bhavcopy** (2000+) | Per-stock futures OI, OI change, basis, rollover %, historical F&O list and lot sizes | Long/short build-up and rollover are watched by every Indian desk; also fixes biases 2.4 |
| Participant OI (have it) | FII/Pro/Client positioning | Market-level regime and exposure scaling |
| **BSE/NSE corporate announcements** (results, orders won, pledges, buybacks, SAST/insider) | Event stream with timestamps | Post-earnings/announcement drift; the best place for a **small fine-tuned text model** |
| Results calendar + reported numbers | Earnings surprise vs history | Post-earnings drift at 20-60 days survives costs |
| Bulk/block deals, F&O ban list, MWPL | Flow and constraint signals | Cheap features and risk filters |

## 6. Recommended architecture: the "smart engine", realistic version
```
Data layer (point-in-time): prices, delivery %, F&O OI/basis, participant OI, events, VIX/global (lagged)
   |
Specialist models (each small, each with its own baseline and IC report):
   1. Cross-sectional alpha ranker (LightGBM, residual-return label, 10-20d)  <- core
   2. Event model: small text classifier fine-tuned on Colab
      (announcement -> type, sentiment, magnitude), labelled by the market-adjusted reaction  <- your "fine-tuned small model"
   3. Volatility forecaster (HAR / LightGBM) -> position sizing and stops
   4. Regime model (VIX, breadth, trend, FII positioning) -> gross exposure 0-100%
   5. Meta-labeller -> take/skip + confidence for each candidate (precision lever)
   6. Execution model on 5-minute data -> entry timing in the first hour
   |
Portfolio + risk (deterministic): beta hedge with Nifty futures, sector caps, lots, margin, ban list, kill switch
   |
Approval UI -> Upstox
```
Footprint: models 1, 3, 4, 5 and 6 are under 10 MB total and run on CPU in milliseconds. Model 2 is a 60-120M-parameter encoder (FinBERT-class), or at most a 0.5-1.5B model quantised to int4, trained on Colab GPU and run on CPU once a day over new announcements. That fits the i5/16 GB machine comfortably. Colab TPU is not useful for any of this; use a Colab **GPU** runtime for the text model only.

## 7. Prioritised next steps (each with a pass/fail gate)
1. **Fix the measuring stick (1-2 days).** Add a fixed baseline set (universe EW, Midcap 100, random-score same construction, 5-day reversal rule), phase-averaged reporting (all start offsets, report mean/min), and factor alpha t-stat. Gate: every future result is reported this way.
2. **Remove the futures biases (2-4 days).** Download the F&O bhavcopy: real futures prices, historical F&O membership and lot sizes, rolls before expiry, no dividends on futures. Re-run the paper config. Gate: phase-mean Sharpe still beats random-score and Midcap 100.
3. **Fill the universe holes.** Build adjusted series for the missing rank-101-200 names from your own bhavcopy plus corporate actions, or restrict the model to top-100 where coverage is 96%. Gate: coverage >95% in the traded universe.
4. **Re-target the model.** Residual (beta/size/sector-neutral) labels at 10 and 20 days; add delivery %, F&O OI build-up, basis, and participant-OI features; evaluate the hedged long-stock/short-Nifty portfolio. Gate: hedged phase-mean Sharpe > 0.5 with alpha t > 2.
5. **Event model (the Colab project).** Collect BSE/NSE announcements with timestamps (start 2018+), label by the next 1-20 day abnormal return, fine-tune a small encoder, and test forward-in-time only. Gate: incremental IC over model 4 with t > 2.
6. **Regime, volatility and meta-labelling layers.** Gate: they lower drawdown or raise precision out-of-sample without overfitting (count them in the DSR).
7. **Paper trading** in parallel from now on, as a plumbing and slippage test with real quotes. Only after 1-6 pass: tiny live, semi-automatic.
